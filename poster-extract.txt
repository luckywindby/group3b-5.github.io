                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               Method Selection                                                                        Summary of Requirements and Pilot Evidence
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       Requirements discovery was based on a short questionnaire distributed to
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       first-year students at the start of semester two (n = 94). The strongest
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       needs were clear route guidance, introduction to important places,
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       and integration of commonly used campus resources. These findings
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       directly shaped the MVP around route-detail guidance, building and
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       service support, and practical-first route entry rather than open
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       exploration alone.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              Impact
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             Campus onboarding is a human-centred challenge, not only a wayfinding challenge. This project matters because existing tools mainly help users move
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             between locations, but do not fully support the early-campus decisions that matter most to newcomers, such as where to begin, which places matter first,
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             and how to connect academic, service, and exploratory needs in one coherent journey. Campus Compass addresses this gap through a guided mobile
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             experience that reduces decision pressure, supports practical first-use needs, and helps users build confidence, familiarity, and belonging on campus.
