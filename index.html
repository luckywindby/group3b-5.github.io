Why We Chose the Social Connectivity Track 为什么我们选择 Social Connectivity 赛道

We chose the Social Connectivity track because the first days on a large university campus are not only a navigation problem but a social transition. New XJTLU students often reach a destination only after uncertainty, repeated asking, or reliance on friends, while visitors and parents experience campus as a sequence of unfamiliar names with little context. We wanted to design something that helps people start conversations with campus itself: not just "how do I get there?", but "where should I begin, what matters first, and how do these places connect to student life?" This track also matched what we observed in route verification and early requirements work. Respondents cared most about clear route guidance, introductions to important places, and integrated support resources, all of which are tied to confidence and belonging rather than efficiency alone. Campus Compass therefore treats navigation as a socially meaningful onboarding experience. It combines route recommendation, important-place explanation, bilingual support, service access, progress feedback, and Campus Memory reflection so users can build familiarity with the campus and feel more settled within it. 我们选择 Social Connectivity 赛道，是因为大型大学校园中的“第一次到校体验”并不只是一个导航问题， 更是一个社会性与适应性过渡过程。西浦新生往往需要在不确定、反复询问或依赖朋友帮助的情况下，才能到达目的地； 而访客和家长看到的则是一连串陌生建筑名称，缺乏清晰语境。我们希望设计的不是一个只回答“怎么走”的工具， 而是一个能帮助用户理解“从哪里开始、什么最重要、这些地点如何与校园生活连接起来”的系统。这个方向也与我们在路线核对、 需求问卷和早期观察中的发现一致：用户最看重的并不是单纯效率，而是清晰路线引导、重要地点介绍以及常用校园资源整合。 因此，Campus Compass 将导航重新定义为一种具有社会意义的校园入门体验。它把路线推荐、重要地点解释、双语支持、服务资源入口、 进度反馈和 Campus Memory 纪念卡整合在一起，帮助用户逐步建立对校园的熟悉感、信心与归属感。

Problem framing 问题界定

Motivation and gap 项目动机与差距

Campus Compass was developed to support campus onboarding, not just campus navigation. For new students and visitors, the challenge is not only reaching a destination, but also knowing where to begin, which places matter first, and how to move through campus with confidence. Campus Compass 的目标并不只是“导航”，而是支持完整的校园 onboarding。对于新生和访客而言， 真正的困难不只是到达某个地点，而是知道应该从哪里开始、哪些地点最重要，以及如何在校园中更有信心地行动。

Existing solutions support navigation, but not onboarding. Commercial maps provide live directions, official campus pages provide factual information, and academic systems improve route structure, yet few combine route priorities, service resources, bilingual guidance, and confidence-building in one mobile journey. 现有方案可以支持“移动”，却无法真正支持“入门”。商业地图提供实时路线，学校官网提供事实信息，学术方案则更关注路线结构与可用性； 但几乎没有系统能在同一个移动体验中同时整合路线优先级、服务资源、双语引导和建立信心的机制。

Need 1: clear route guidance 需求 1：清晰的路线引导 Need 2: important-place introduction 需求 2：重要地点介绍 Need 3: integrated campus resources 需求 3：整合常用校园资源

Academic papers 学术论文

Paetow et al. (2021)

  • Did well: requirement-driven campus navigation design. 优点：以需求为起点的校园导航设计。
  • Did well: kept the university context central. 优点：始终围绕大学校园场景展开。
  • Missed: less focus on emotion, onboarding, and belonging. 不足：对情感、入门过程和归属感关注不足。

Olaleye et al. (2024)

  • Did well: accessibility and inclusive navigation support. 优点：重视无障碍与包容性导航。
  • Did well: usability taken seriously as evaluation evidence. 优点：把可用性评估作为重要证据。
  • Missed: limited playful motivation and mixed-user onboarding. 不足：缺少趣味性激励和混合用户 onboarding 的考虑。

Tahir & Krogstie (2023)

  • Did well: studied workload and hesitation in campus wayfinding. 优点：关注校园寻路中的认知负担与犹豫。
  • Did well: compared aids in real campus tasks. 优点：在真实校园任务中比较导航辅助方式。
  • Missed: little onboarding guidance and service-resource integration. 不足：缺少 onboarding 指导和服务资源整合视角。

Li et al. (2021)

  • Did well: explored AR-enhanced campus navigation possibilities. 优点：探索了 AR 强化校园导航的可能性。
  • Did well: highlighted richer spatial interaction. 优点：强调更丰富的空间交互体验。
  • Missed: limited practical onboarding and reflection loop. 不足：实用入门引导和反思闭环不足。
Commercial products 商业产品

AMap

  • Did well: reliable live route handoff and familiar mobile patterns. 优点：实时导航接力可靠，移动端交互成熟。
  • Missed: no newcomer prioritisation or campus explanation. 不足：没有新手优先级和校园语境解释。

Google Maps

  • Did well: strong generic A-to-B navigation model. 优点：通用 A-to-B 导航能力非常强。
  • Missed: campus touring becomes a plain street-map task. 不足：校园导览会被简化成普通街道地图任务。

MazeMap

  • Did well: campus-specific indoor-outdoor orientation. 优点：更接近校园场景的室内外衔接导航。
  • Missed: weaker emotional onboarding and playful reflection. 不足：在情感 onboarding 和趣味反思方面较弱。

Apple Maps

  • Did well: clean mobile-first route visibility. 优点：移动端路线可视化清晰、干净。
  • Missed: no service, route-family, or campus-memory layers. 不足：没有服务资源、路线家族和记忆沉淀层。

Journey synthesis 旅程提炼

Pain points and opportunities 痛点与设计机会

  • Before navigation, users struggle most with where to begin and which route matters first. 导航前最大的困难是：从哪里开始，以及哪条路线最重要。
  • During navigation, unfamiliar building codes and entrances create hesitation. 导航中，陌生建筑代号和入口会引发犹豫。
  • After navigation, closure matters: completion, feedback, and memory make the route meaningful. 导航后，完成感很重要：完成反馈、评价和记忆沉淀会让路线更有意义。
The opportunity 设计机会 Reduce decision pressure before movement, make recommendation transparent, keep mobile progress visible, and extend arrival into reflection and belonging. 在行动前降低决策压力，让推荐更透明，保证移动端进度可见，并把“到达”延伸为“反思与归属感建立”。

Playful must-haves “具备趣味性”必须满足的条件

The product must remain practical first, but still feel playful and human-centred. 产品必须以实用为核心，同时具有趣味性和人本关怀。

1. Visible progress and closure 1. 可见的进度与完成感

Playfulness should include route completion, progress feedback, and a clear ending. 趣味性不只是装饰，而应包括路线进度、完成反馈和明确的结束仪式感。

2. Meaningful discovery 2. 有意义的探索

Users should discover stories, landmarks, seasonal elements, or hidden spots that make the campus memorable. 用户应该能发现故事、地标、季节性内容或 hidden spots，使校园体验更可记忆。

3. Lightweight rewards and memory 3. 轻量奖励与记忆沉淀

Badges, postcard export, and route history should reinforce motivation without overwhelming users. 徽章、明信片导出和路线历史应强化动力，而不是增加额外负担。

Practical onboarding + playful belonging 实用 onboarding + 趣味归属感

Campus Compass reframes campus touring as practical onboarding plus playful belonging, rather than a generic map task. Design implication 设计启示

What the evaluation implies 评估说明了什么

  • Route entry should stay recommendation-led because first-use demand is practical and guided.路线入口应继续以推荐为主，因为首次使用需求明显偏向实用和被引导。
  • Important-place explanation is part of usefulness, not optional decoration.重要地点解释不是装饰，而是实用性的一部分。
  • Campus support tools should stay in the main flow, not hidden behind secondary pages.校园支持工具应保留在主流程中，而不是藏在次级页面里。
  • Memory and badges work best when they reinforce the practical core.徽章和记忆沉淀在服务于实用核心时效果最好。

Iterative refinement 迭代优化

One major change was moving from a browse-heavy start toward guided hybrid route entry. 一个关键迭代是：从偏浏览式首页，转向更具引导性的混合路线入口。

Before改动前

Browse-first entry created decision pressure先浏览再选择会带来更大决策压力

Reconstructed from the discarded early direction, this browse-heavy start asked users to decode route families before stating a real goal such as going to class, finding services, or visiting campus. For first-time freshmen and visitors, this increased hesitation and cognitive load at the exact moment they needed reassurance. It was later replaced by a recommendation-led hybrid entry that lets users begin from need, not from taxonomy. 这张图根据被放弃的早期方向重构而成：用户必须先理解“路线家族”分类，才能表达自己真实的任务，例如去上课、找服务点或带访客参观。对于首次使用的新生和访客来说，这会在最需要被引导的时候增加犹豫和认知负担。后来我们改成了以推荐为主的混合式入口，让用户从“需求”出发，而不是从“分类”出发。

After改动后

Hybrid route entry supports confidence and flexibility混合式入口同时支持信心建立与灵活性

The implemented flow now combines a clear recommendation entry, route-mode cards, and practical shortcuts for buildings, services, printers, and visitor-style exploration. This lowers first-use decision pressure because users can act on an immediate need first, then browse further only if they want to. It also aligns more closely with the requirement findings that users valued clear route guidance and practical support more strongly than open-ended exploration alone. 实现后的流程把清晰的推荐入口、路线模式卡片以及教学楼、服务点、打印和 visitor 式探索等实用快捷入口结合在一起。这样用户可以先回应眼前的真实需求，再决定是否继续浏览和探索，从而显著降低首次使用时的决策压力。这也更符合需求调研结果：用户对清晰路线引导和实用支持的重视程度，高于单纯开放式探索。
