The Why | around 200 words 选择原因 | 约 200 词内容

Why We Chose the Social Connectivity Track 为什么我们选择 Social Connectivity 赛道

We chose the Social Connectivity track because the first days on a large university campus are not only a navigation problem but a social transition. New XJTLU students often reach a destination only after uncertainty, repeated asking, or reliance on friends, while visitors and parents experience campus as a sequence of unfamiliar names with little context. We wanted to design something that helps people start conversations with campus itself: not just "how do I get there?", but "where should I begin, what matters first, and how do these places connect to student life?" This track also matched what we observed in route verification and early requirements work. Respondents cared most about clear route guidance, introductions to important places, and integrated support resources, all of which are tied to confidence and belonging rather than efficiency alone. Campus Compass therefore treats navigation as a socially meaningful onboarding experience. It combines route recommendation, important-place explanation, bilingual support, service access, progress feedback, and Campus Memory reflection so users can build familiarity with the campus and feel more settled within it. 我们选择 Social Connectivity 赛道，是因为大型大学校园中的“第一次到校体验”并不只是一个导航问题， 更是一个社会性与适应性过渡过程。西浦新生往往需要在不确定、反复询问或依赖朋友帮助的情况下，才能到达目的地； 而访客和家长看到的则是一连串陌生建筑名称，缺乏清晰语境。我们希望设计的不是一个只回答“怎么走”的工具， 而是一个能帮助用户理解“从哪里开始、什么最重要、这些地点如何与校园生活连接起来”的系统。这个方向也与我们在路线核对、 需求问卷和早期观察中的发现一致：用户最看重的并不是单纯效率，而是清晰路线引导、重要地点介绍以及常用校园资源整合。 因此，Campus Compass 将导航重新定义为一种具有社会意义的校园入门体验。它把路线推荐、重要地点解释、双语支持、服务资源入口、 进度反馈和 Campus Memory 纪念卡整合在一起，帮助用户逐步建立对校园的熟悉感、信心与归属感。

Problem framing 问题界定

Motivation and gap 项目动机与差距

Campus Compass was developed to support campus onboarding, not just campus navigation. For new students and visitors, the challenge is not only reaching a destination, but also knowing where to begin, which places matter first, and how to move through campus with confidence. Campus Compass 的目标并不只是“导航”，而是支持完整的校园 onboarding。对于新生和访客而言， 真正的困难不只是到达某个地点，而是知道应该从哪里开始、哪些地点最重要，以及如何在校园中更有信心地行动。

Existing solutions support navigation, but not onboarding. Commercial maps provide live directions, official campus pages provide factual information, and academic systems improve route structure, yet few combine route priorities, service resources, bilingual guidance, and confidence-building in one mobile journey. 现有方案可以支持“移动”，却无法真正支持“入门”。商业地图提供实时路线，学校官网提供事实信息，学术方案则更关注路线结构与可用性； 但几乎没有系统能在同一个移动体验中同时整合路线优先级、服务资源、双语引导和建立信心的机制。

Need 1: clear route guidance 需求 1：清晰的路线引导 Need 2: important-place introduction 需求 2：重要地点介绍 Need 3: integrated campus resources 需求 3：整合常用校园资源

Academic papers 学术论文

Paetow et al. (2021)

  • Focused on: Designing a university navigation app through participatory workshops, requirement analysis, and prototype evaluation. 研究焦点：通过参与式工作坊、需求分析与原型评估设计大学导航应用。
  • Did well: Clearly shows how user needs translate into features such as routing, POI search, and room-related services. 优点：清晰呈现用户需求如何转化为路线规划、POI 搜索和房间服务等功能。
  • Missed: Focuses more on system design efficiency than on first-use onboarding, emotional support, or helping new students adapt. 不足：更侧重系统设计效率，较少关注初次使用引导、情感支持或新生适应需求。
  • Our response: We adopt its structured design logic, but extend it beyond navigation to support onboarding, practical campus guidance, and a smoother first-campus experience. 我们的回应：借鉴其结构化设计逻辑，并将其延伸至 onboarding、校园实用指引和更流畅的首次入校体验。

Olaleye et al. (2024)

  • Focused on: Testing an assistive campus navigation system for visually impaired students using real-world trials, interviews, and questionnaires. 研究焦点：通过真实场景测试、访谈与问卷评估面向视障学生的辅助校园导航系统。
  • Did well: Highlights the value of accessibility, independence, safety, and real-use evaluation in campus navigation design. 优点：强调无障碍性、独立性、安全性与真实使用评估在校园导航设计中的价值。
  • Missed: Its context is assistive hardware for visually impaired users, so it is less relevant to mainstream web-based onboarding and general student/visitor use. 不足：聚焦视障辅助硬件场景，对主流 Web 端 onboarding 及普通学生/访客使用的参考有限。
  • Our response: It reminds us to make Campus Compass more inclusive by considering accessibility, multimodal guidance, and practical support in real campus conditions. 我们的回应：提醒我们在真实校园条件下，通过无障碍设计、多模态引导与实用支持，让 Campus Compass 更具包容性。

Tahir & Krogstie (2023)

  • Focused on: Comparing navigation aids and examining how spatial ability and workload affect campus wayfinding performance. 研究焦点：比较导航辅助方式，研究空间能力与认知负荷如何影响校园寻路表现。
  • Did well: Shows that successful campus navigation depends not only on route accuracy, but also on cognitive load, seamless indoor-outdoor support, and user wayfinding challenges. 优点：揭示成功的校园导航不仅取决于路线准确性，还受认知负荷、室内外无缝衔接及用户寻路挑战的影响。
  • Missed: Pays less attention to emotional onboarding, early campus adaptation, and the needs of first-time student users. 不足：较少关注情感性 onboarding、早期校园适应以及初次到访学生的需求。
  • Our response: We respond by designing Campus Compass to reduce confusion and effort through clearer entry points, guided routes, and practical support for first-time campus navigation. 我们的回应：通过更清晰的入口、引导路线和实用支持，设计 Campus Compass 以降低初次使用的困惑与操作负担。

Li et al. (2021)

  • Focused on: Using AR to combine navigation with real-time building information, cultural context, and visitor-oriented campus exploration. 研究焦点：利用 AR 将导航与实时建筑信息、文化背景和面向访客的校园探索相结合。
  • Did well: Shows that campus navigation can be more than route guidance; it can also deliver contextual and engaging information. 优点：展示校园导航不仅是路线引导，还可传递情境化的丰富信息。
  • Missed: More technology-driven and less focused on usability evidence, onboarding needs, or everyday student support. 不足：更侧重技术驱动，较少关注可用性证据、onboarding 需求或日常学生支持。
  • Our response: We take from it the idea that navigation should also explain the campus, not just guide movement, and use this to enrich onboarding and campus understanding. 我们的回应：借鉴其理念——导航应解释校园，而不仅是引导移动，并以此丰富 onboarding 体验与校园认知。
Commercial products 商业产品

Google Maps

  • Did well: Provides efficient and familiar route planning; clear turn-by-turn guidance with very low learning cost; strong baseline for A-to-B navigation. 优点：高效易用的路线规划；逐向导航清晰，学习成本极低；是 A 到 B 导航的强力基础工具。
  • Missed: Lacks campus-specific semantic information; limited room-level indoor navigation; no onboarding or exploration-oriented guidance; does not integrate practical support resources. 不足：缺乏校园专属语义信息；房间级室内导航支持有限；不支持首次使用引导或探索式校园了解；未将实用支持资源整合进路线体验。
  • Our response: Campus Compass extends beyond "getting there" by supporting campus-specific onboarding, practical support, and beginner-friendly exploration — helping users understand where to begin, what key places mean, and which route fits their situation. 我们的回应：Campus Compass 超越"到达目的地"，提供校园专属引导、实用支持与新手友好探索——帮助用户了解从哪里开始、关键地点的含义以及哪条路线最适合自己。

MazeMap

  • Did well: Detailed indoor navigation across buildings and rooms; integrated indoor–outdoor transitions; clearer step-by-step guidance; improved building and room search. 优点：支持跨建筑与房间的详细室内导航；整合室内外过渡；分步指导更清晰；建筑与房间搜索优于纯室外导航工具。
  • Missed: Mainly goal-oriented navigation, not onboarding or exploration; limited personalization for different user roles; no integration of practical campus support resources; still poses challenges around entry points, floor layouts, and cognitive load. 不足：仍以目标导向导航为主，缺乏引导与探索功能；不同用户角色（如新生与访客）的个性化支持有限；未整合实用校园支持资源；仍在入口、楼层布局及认知负荷方面存在挑战。
  • Our response: Building on MazeMap's indoor–outdoor continuity, Campus Compass shifts the focus from pure wayfinding to guided onboarding — users can enter from different needs, receive beginner-friendly route choices, and access practical support through one connected experience. 我们的回应：在 MazeMap 室内外衔接的基础上，Campus Compass 将重心从纯寻路转向引导式 onboarding——用户可按不同需求进入，获得新手友好的路线选择，并通过统一体验获取实用支持。

Official Campus App / Website 官方校园应用 / 网站

  • Did well: Comprehensive campus information in one place; supports multiple user needs beyond navigation; trusted official source. 优点：在一处提供全面的校园信息；满足导航之外的多种用户需求；作为官方内容的可靠来源。
  • Missed: Information organized for search/reference, not first-time onboarding; navigation and information often separated; no guided map experience connecting routes, campus meaning, and support; assumes users already understand campus structure. 不足：信息为搜索/查阅而组织，不适合初次使用；导航与信息通常分离；缺乏将路线、校园意义与实用支持相连的引导式地图体验；默认用户已熟悉校园结构和术语。
  • Our response: Campus Compass restructures campus content into a guided, action-oriented experience — turning static information into tour-oriented and task-oriented guidance that helps users quickly understand the campus while moving through it. 我们的回应：Campus Compass 将校园内容重构为引导式、行动导向的体验——将静态信息转化为游览与任务导向的指导，帮助用户在穿行校园的同时快速建立认知。

Duolingo / Nike Run Club Duolingo / Nike Run Club

  • Did well: Clear progress tracking and reward loops; sense of momentum and achievement; repeated interaction feels lightweight and encouraging rather than purely functional. 优点：清晰的进度跟踪与奖励机制；给用户带来动力感和成就感；重复互动感觉轻松愉快而非纯功能性操作。
  • Missed: Engagement systems not designed for spatial navigation or campus exploration; gamification can become the main experience rather than a support layer; do not address practical wayfinding challenges in campus environments. 不足：互动系统并非为空间导航或校园探索而设计；游戏化元素可能喧宾夺主成为主体体验；未解决校园环境中寻路的实际与认知挑战。
  • Our response: These products inform our engagement strategy, but we apply gamification in a lighter and more supportive way — engaging elements in Campus Compass encourage exploration and familiarity while staying secondary to practical guidance, route clarity, and onboarding support. 我们的回应：这些产品为我们的互动策略提供参考，但我们以更轻量、更具辅助性的方式应用游戏化——Campus Compass 中的互动元素旨在鼓励探索与熟悉，始终服务于实用指引、路线清晰度和 onboarding 支持。

Journey synthesis 旅程提炼

Pain points and opportunities 痛点与设计机会

  • Before navigation, users struggle most with where to begin and which route matters first. 导航前最大的困难是：从哪里开始，以及哪条路线最重要。
  • During navigation, unfamiliar building codes and entrances create hesitation. 导航中，陌生建筑代号和入口会引发犹豫。

It also aligns more closely with the requirement findings that users valued clear route guidance and practical support more strongly than open-ended exploration alone. 实现后的流程把清晰的推荐入口、路线模式卡片以及教学楼、服务点、打印和 visitor 式探索等实用快捷入口结合在一起。这样用户可以先回应眼前的真实需求，再决定是否继续浏览和探索，从而显著降低首次使用时的决策压力。这也更符合需求调研结果：用户对清晰路线引导和实用支持的重视程度，高于单纯开放式探索。
