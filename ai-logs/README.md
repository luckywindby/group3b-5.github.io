# AI Polishing Notes for `web/index.html`

This folder documents how AI was used to help organise, polish, revise, and check the portfolio page `web/index.html`.
It is included inside the `web` folder so the submitted portfolio can provide its own AI-use evidence without depending on files outside the web package.

Scope note: these files are specifically about AI assistance in organising, reviewing, polishing, and checking `web/index.html`. The portfolio page itself also discloses broader AI use in the Campus Compass system, such as DeepSeek-assisted Campus Memory copy.

## Files

- `primary-prompts.md`: cleaned record of the main AI-assisted prompts used to structure, polish, revise, and check `web/index.html`.

## AI assistance used for `web/index.html`

- Portfolio structure planning: deciding how to organise motivation, requirements, ideation, implementation, contribution, evaluation, reflection, AI disclosure, and references.
- Bilingual wording support: polishing English-Chinese explanations while keeping the final meaning checked by the author.
- HTML/CSS/JS review: checking section links, resource paths, script syntax, image alt text, and submission-readiness issues.
- AI reflection review: checking whether the existing AI-use disclosure clearly followed the required P/O/A structure.
- Consistency checking: aligning the portfolio narrative with poster evidence, implemented features, route-verification photos, alpha-test records, and AI-log links.

## Human control statement

AI was used as a polishing, review, and diagnostic tool for the portfolio page, not as the final author or final decision-maker. All suggestions were reviewed, edited, and accepted manually before submission.

## What AI did not replace

- AI did not collect questionnaire data, run interviews, take route-verification photos, or conduct alpha testing.
- AI did not decide which claims were true. Claims in `web/index.html` were checked against the poster, implemented system, evidence images, alpha-test records, and local files.
- AI did not produce the final portfolio independently. It suggested structure, wording, and checks, but the author selected, edited, rejected, or reworked the outputs.
- AI did not invent statistics or user feedback. Numbers such as `n = 94`, `n = 21`, and `n = 3` were taken from project evidence and then explained in the portfolio.

## Reflection focus

The main value of AI was that it helped reveal gaps in the portfolio, such as when the AI-use reflection was too general and did not yet follow the required P/O/A structure. The final action was human-led: we changed the page, narrowed claims to available evidence, and kept only content that could be justified.

## Evidence boundary

This is a cleaned AI-use log rather than a full chat transcript. It records the main types of AI help used for `web/index.html`, what worked, what needed correction, and what human action followed.
